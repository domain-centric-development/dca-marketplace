# Words on the page

The detail of the skill's section 12.

- **Plain language, in the user's words**: a label says what happens or what something is, not what the code calls
  it. A heading that needs a glossary is a rewrite.
- **One term, one meaning** across labels, states, help and the browser tests.
- **Structure through layout, not through numbering**: indent, heading level and grouping show what belongs
  together.
- **A number says what it counts** ("3 checks"), or it is left out; no decoration (symbols, glued counts) in
  headings and labels.
