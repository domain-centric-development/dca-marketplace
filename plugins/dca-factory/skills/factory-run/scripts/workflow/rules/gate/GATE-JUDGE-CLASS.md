# GATE-JUDGE-CLASS

**When:** the document gate runs, over the confirmed findings of the judge's verdict, from contract 39

**Why:** a lesson counts findings by their class; a finding described in free words alone — "duplicate fixture",
"copied helper", "same setup again" — is three keys for one pattern and counts in none. Three short closed lists,
picked by the judge from what it confirmed: what had to change, the kind of correction, and whether something was
missing, wrong or too much. Experimental: the gate says it and lets the story pass, until the judges keep to it.
